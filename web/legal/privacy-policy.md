# Privacy Policy

CURA Software LLC

Last Updated: June 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CURA Software LLC ("CURA," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use the CURA mobile application and related services (the "Service"). By using CURA, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

- Email address
- Username or profile name
- Encrypted authentication credentials

### 1.2 Location Data

Location data is central to how CURA works. We collect precise location information to enable core features of the Service, including:

- GPS coordinates
- Geofencing data (entry and exit of approved study locations)
- Time-stamped location data during study sessions

We use this data to:

- Verify your presence at approved study locations
- Track study session duration
- Detect and prevent fraud, spoofing, or manipulation
- Calculate rewards, points, and progress

### Background Location

CURA collects location data in the background only while an active study session is in progress. Background location access stops immediately when your session ends. You can disable location permissions at any time in your device settings, but doing so will limit or disable core features of the Service.

### 1.3 Usage Data

We collect information about how you interact with the Service, including:

- Study session duration and frequency
- Feature usage and interaction patterns
- App performance data
- Reward and level progression activity

### 1.4 Device Information

We may collect device-level information for security and fraud prevention purposes, including:

- Device model and operating system version
- App version
- Device identifiers

## 2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Provide and operate the Service
- Verify study activity and location-based engagement
- Calculate rewards, points, and user progression
- Prevent fraud, abuse, and misuse
- Improve app performance and user experience
- Provide customer support

## 3. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal data. We only share information in the following limited circumstances.

### 3.1 With Partner Businesses (Rewards System)

CURA partners with third-party businesses to provide rewards. We may share reward eligibility status and confirmation that requirements were met (such as study minutes achieved). We do NOT share real-time location data, individual GPS trails, or raw location history with partners.

### 3.2 Aggregated and Anonymized Analytics

We may share aggregated, anonymized data with partner businesses. This may include:

- Total visits to a location over a given time period
- Aggregate study session durations at a location
- General engagement trends such as peak usage times
- Reward redemption statistics

This data does not identify individual users and does not include names, email addresses, account identifiers, or raw location history.

### 3.3 Service Providers

We may share data with trusted third-party providers that assist in operating the Service, such as cloud hosting, analytics, and security vendors. These providers are contractually limited to using data only to perform services on our behalf.

### 3.4 Legal Compliance

We may disclose information when required to comply with applicable laws or legal processes, enforce our Terms or policies, or protect safety, security, or prevent fraud.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

### 4.1 Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide the Service, maintain account functionality, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes or prevent abuse.

### 4.2 Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by emailing us at:

info@cura.coffee

Upon receiving a verified deletion request, we will:

- Delete or anonymize your personal account data
- Remove identifiers linked to your account
- Stop processing location and usage data tied to your account

We will complete verified deletion requests within 45 days, as permitted under applicable law. We may retain limited data where required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or maintain truly anonymized aggregated analytics that cannot identify you.

## 5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information, including encryption of data in transit. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of data transmitted or stored.

## 6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to withdraw location permissions at any time. California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what data we collect, the right to delete, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal data (we do not sell personal data).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at:

info@cura.coffee

## 7. Children's Privacy

CURA is intended for users 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 18. If we become aware that we have collected data from a minor, we will delete it promptly.

## 8. Third-Party Services

The Service may integrate with or link to third-party services that have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties, and we encourage you to review their policies before use.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last Updated" date when changes are made. For material changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify you through the app or via email prior to the change taking effect. Continued use of CURA after updates constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
