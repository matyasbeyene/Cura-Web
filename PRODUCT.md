# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Cura Web serves two groups: students exploring Cura from the public site, and partner business owners using the dashboard. The dashboard user is a business owner or manager who wants a clear read on student visits, focus time, and reward activity without needing to understand raw analytics.

## Product Purpose

Cura Web is the public site and business dashboard for Cura, a study-focus iOS app. The dashboard gives each signed-in partner business a simple, privacy-safe view of how students use its space and lets the owner manage the rewards connected to that business.

## Brand Personality

Calm, useful, and trustworthy. The product should feel warm enough for local cafes and study spots, but precise enough that business owners trust the numbers and controls.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS chrome, decorative dashboards, dense enterprise admin panels, and playful reward UI that makes business tools feel unserious. Avoid adding parallel business or reward models outside the canonical `study_locations -> businesses -> offers` data path.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the business owner in flow with clear summaries and direct actions.
2. Prefer plain language over analytics jargon.
3. Show enough detail to make decisions without exposing private student data.
4. Make common actions visible, reversible where possible, and hard to trigger by accident.
5. Stay aligned with Cura's existing warm, restrained visual system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, visible labels, clear focus and disabled states, keyboard-friendly controls, and explicit confirmation for destructive actions. Dashboard copy should remain easy to scan and easy to understand.
